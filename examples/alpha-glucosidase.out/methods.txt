Molecular docking was performed against alpha-glucosidase (PDB 3A4A, 1.6
A), Saccharomyces cerevisiae isomaltase used as a structural surrogate for
human intestinal alpha-glucosidase. The receptor was prepared raw cleaned
3A4A, heteroatoms and waters removed, Vina assigns atom types. Ligands
were prepared with rdkit ETKDGv3 + MMFF94, meeko 0.7.1 at pH 7.4. The
search box measured 30 x 30 x 30 A centred at (21.52, -7.70, 23.55), on
GLC 601. The box encloses Asp215, Glu277, Asp352. Docking used AutoDock
Vina 1.2.7; exhaustiveness 8; 9 modes; energy range 3 kcal/mol; seed 42. A
single seed was used, so reported values are reproducible rather than
replicate means. Redocking of GLC 601 on a receptor prepared meeko, polar
H and Gasteiger charges, in a 18 A box at exhaustiveness 16 gave redock
RMSD 0.52 A, within the accepted 2.0 A criterion. This control was run
under a different receptor preparation from the screen, which used raw
cleaned 3A4A, heteroatoms and waters removed, Vina assigns atom types, and
it therefore validates pose recovery rather than the screening protocol
itself. Compounds were selected at the level of genus Ziziphus; they are
candidate compounds documented across the genus rather than isolated from
a single species, and binding is predicted in silico rather than
demonstrated. A same-seed replication of 5 compounds through TalanaiDock
(vina 1.2.7, 3A4A, seed 42) agreed to within 0.021 kcal/mol.
